Shorts Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool that XyzinNyan runs on their own computer. It uploads videos they made to their own YouTube channel. It isn't offered to the public.

What it accesses

When you sign in, the tool asks Google for one permission: uploading videos to your YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It doesn't read your existing videos, comments, analytics, email or any other Google account data.

What it stores

It saves one sign-in token in a file (youtube_token.json) on the computer where it runs, so it can upload without asking you to sign in every time. The video files, titles, descriptions and tags stay on that computer and are only sent to YouTube when a video is uploaded.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or sent anywhere except Google/YouTube. The tool has no analytics, cookies or ads.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can remove the tool's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page. Deleting youtube_token.json from the computer removes the only data the tool stores.
