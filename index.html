Shorts Uploader

Shorts Uploader is a small personal tool that XyzinNyan runs on their own computer. It renders the animated shorts they make in Blender and uploads them to their own YouTube channel once they've reviewed and approved each one.

It isn't a public service. It only asks Google for permission to upload videos, and only to the channel the owner signs in with.
